RentCover

Will the rent pay your mortgage, and how much is left over each month?

How it works

  1. Tell it the price, ZIP and bedrooms, plus your down payment and rate if you know them. Pasting the listing text works too.
  2. It pulls local rent from US Census data and this week's mortgage rate from Freddie Mac.
  3. It estimates repairs from size, age and condition, and compares a standard renovation at median rent with a luxury renovation at top quarter rent, side by side.
  4. It lines up rent against the mortgage, taxes, insurance, vacancy and upkeep to show what's left each month.
  5. It runs 5,000 simulations of lower rent, vacancy and cost spikes to show how likely you are to come up short, and how much cash you'd need if things go wrong.

Try one

Educational tool, not financial advice. Census rents are medians that lag the market; the national mortgage rate is adjusted up for rental loans.
